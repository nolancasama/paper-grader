## Print use

- Apply **only to confirmed 100/100 tests**, alongside the separate illustrated chibi **PERFECT!** stamp.
- The assets contain **transparent centers** (no worksheet background); overlay onto the marks-only correction PDF and preview.
- SVG viewBox is **1122 × 794**, landscape A4 proportion. Maintain original scan page orientation and **normal-order** print mapping, rotating art for scanned upside-down physical originals as needed.
- Scale to the physical page at **100%**, adjust placement and trim/simplify decorations to fit the verified printer-safe margin on RISO ORPHIS GL7430; avoid answers, name and score boxes. Border drawing should stay narrow and light, approximately 3–5 mm in the intended print-size version.
- Render and visually check **every** final 100/100 page with its border and mascot before printing onto student originals.
- Teacher has not yet specified whether A, B, or C should be the sole default or whether the three should rotate between perfect scores. Do not assume a mix is locked in.
